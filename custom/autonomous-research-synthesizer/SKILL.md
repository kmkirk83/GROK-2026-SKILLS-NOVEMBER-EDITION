# Autonomous Research Synthesizer

Long-horizon multi-source research with source quality scoring, contradiction detection, and structured synthesis for high-stakes decisions.

## Core Protocol
1. Define research questions and success criteria.
2. Collect multi-source evidence with quality scoring.
3. Detect contradictions and gaps.
4. Synthesize findings into actionable structured output.
5. Cite sources and confidence levels.
