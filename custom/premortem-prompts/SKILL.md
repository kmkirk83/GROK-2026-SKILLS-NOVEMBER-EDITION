# Premortem Prompts

## Overview

A disciplined five-step process that assumes failure has already occurred and works backward to surface hidden risks, strengthen the plan, and install early-warning tripwires.

## Five Steps

1. **Assume Failure** — Write the future failure story in vivid detail.
2. **Rank Failure Modes** — Identify and prioritize the most probable and impactful causes.
3. **Rebuild the Plan** — Modify the original plan to neutralize the top risks.
4. **Simulate Adversaries** — Role-play competitors, regulators, customers, and internal critics.
5. **Install Tripwires** — Define measurable early indicators and response actions.

## Output

Produce a concise premortem report containing the ranked risks, revised plan elements, adversary insights, and tripwire list.
